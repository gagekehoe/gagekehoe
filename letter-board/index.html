Letter Board

A calm AAC letter board for spelling one letter at a time.

What it is

Letter Board is an augmentative and alternative communication (AAC) app. You spell a message one letter at a time on large landscape keys. A one-line message strip sits above the board. The board includes Speak, Space, Backspace, Clear, a period, and a question mark. Speak uses the device’s own text-to-speech.

Who it is for

It is for the person communicating, and for family members, educators, and clinicians who stay with them. Letter Board is a communication tool. Clinical decisions stay with the person and their clinicians.

Platforms

Letter Board works on iPhone, iPad, and Android phones and tablets. No account or sign-in is required.
