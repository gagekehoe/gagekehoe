How to reach a person

Email gagekehoe17@gmail.com for help with Letter Board. Include your device and the app version, and leave out your password.

When you write, include the page or task you were on, what you expected, your device, and the app version from the store. Say whether the question is about installing, day-to-day use, privacy, or removing local data. Leave out passwords. Send private messages only when you mean to share them.

If someone is relying on the app to communicate while you sort out a problem, keep another way to communicate nearby. A paper letter board, a person who knows them, or another device already in use can cover the gap.
