Support

Help for Letter Board. The App Store Support URL for this app is https://www.gagekehoe.com. This page is the detailed support address on that site.

How to reach a person

A support email is not listed on this page yet. Use the developer contact shown on the Apple App Store or Google Play listing for Letter Board.

When you write, include the page or task you were on, what you expected, your device, and the app version from the store. Say whether the question is about installing, day-to-day use, privacy, or removing local data. Leave out passwords. Send private messages only when you mean to share them.

If someone is relying on the app to communicate while you sort out a problem, keep another way to communicate nearby. A paper letter board, a person who knows them, or another device already in use can cover the gap.

Questions people ask

What is Letter Board?

A calm AAC letter board for spelling one letter at a time. Large landscape keys fill most of the screen. A one-line message strip sits above the board. Controls include Speak, Space, Backspace, Clear, a period, and a question mark. Speak uses on-device text-to-speech.

Where do I download Letter Board?

From the Apple App Store (iPhone and iPad) or Google Play (Android) once that listing is public. This website does not host an install file. Links to the listings will be placed on the Letter Board overview when they exist.

Who is the app for?

People who use augmentative and alternative communication, and the families and clinicians supporting them. It is a communication tool. It is not a medical service, and it does not replace a clinician’s judgment.

Does the app need an account?

No. Letter Board does not require an account. You can use it without signing in.

Does Letter Board collect my data?

No. The app does not collect personal data. The message you spell stays on your device. Speak uses the device’s own text-to-speech and does not send your message to a remote speech service. See the privacy policy.

The app is not behaving as expected. What should I try?

Install the latest version from the store you used, then restart the device. If it is still stuck, write to support with the device and the version. Keep a backup way to communicate while you wait.

How do I remove data from the app?

Letter Board does not create an account or store your messages on a server. Clear the message with Clear, or delete the app to remove anything stored only on that device. See the deletion page.

Is this website the app?

No. These pages describe Letter Board and hold the policies the stores ask for. Communication happens in the app.
