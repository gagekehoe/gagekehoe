Last updated 8 November 2026.

Who is responsible

A company legal name is not published on this site. Gage Kehoe is the developer named for Letter Board.

Questions use the contact on the support page.

This website

These pages are static files. They do not ask you to sign in. They do not set tracking cookies. They do not load third-party analytics, advertising, or fonts. The host that serves the files may keep ordinary connection logs, such as IP address, date, and browser type, to operate and protect the site. Those logs belong to the host. They are not collected by a script on these pages.

Information the app handles

Letter Board does not collect personal data. It does not create an account, and it does not send your message to a Letter Board server.

  • Communication content you enter stays on your device so you can spell and speak a message.
  • A purchase or download record may be held by Apple or Google if you get the app through a store. This website does not receive your payment card number.

This policy does not claim that information is encrypted in a particular way, stored in a particular country, or covered by a named certification.

Speak

Speak uses the device’s built-in text-to-speech. Your message is spoken on the device. Letter Board does not send that text to a remote speech service operated by the publisher.

Analytics and other services

This website does not use analytics scripts. Letter Board does not use a third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting service named on this page. If a later version adds one, this policy will name it.

Accounts

Letter Board does not require an account and does not offer one. This website is not a login.

Children

Letter Board may be used by children. A parent or guardian should decide what is entered about a child and should read this policy first. Because the app does not collect personal data, there is no child account held by the publisher to delete. To remove content on a device, clear the message in the app or delete the app.

Sharing and how long information is kept

Information is not sold. The publisher does not receive personal data from the app to share. Apple or Google may process download or purchase information under that store’s own policy. A support message you choose to send includes whatever you write.

Content that stays on your device remains until you clear it or delete the app.

Deletion

There is no Letter Board account to close. Steps for removing on-device content are on the deletion page.

Changes and contact

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. The page at this address is the current version.
