Delete an account or your data

Letter Board does not require an account and does not create one. There is no publisher-held account to close. This page explains how to remove content that stays only on your device.

No account with the publisher

Letter Board does not collect personal data and does not store your messages on a Letter Board server. You do not need to email the publisher to “delete an account,” because there is none.

Delete data stored only on your device

What you type stays on that device until you clear it or remove the app.

  1. Open Letter Board and tap Clear to erase the message strip, or
  2. Open the device settings that list installed apps, find Letter Board, and clear the app’s storage or delete the app.

That removes the copy on that device. It does not reach any other device you used. Apple or Google may keep their own records, such as a purchase receipt, under their policies.
