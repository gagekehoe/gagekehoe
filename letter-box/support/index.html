Support

Help for Letter Box AAC. This is the support address for the Apple App Store and Google Play listings.

How to reach a person

A support email is not listed on this page yet. Use the developer contact shown on the Apple App Store or Google Play listing for Letter Box AAC.

When you write, include the page or task you were on, what you expected, your device, and the app version from the store. Say whether the question is about installing, day-to-day use, privacy, or deletion. Leave out passwords. Send private messages only when you mean to share them.

If someone is relying on the app to communicate while you sort out a problem, keep another way to communicate nearby. A paper letter board, a person who knows them, or another device already in use can cover the gap.

Questions people ask

Where do I download Letter Box AAC?

From the Apple App Store or Google Play once that listing is public. This website does not host an install file. Links to the listings will be placed on the Letter Box AAC overview when they exist.

Who is the app for?

People who use augmentative and alternative communication, and the families and clinicians supporting them. It is a communication tool. It is not a medical service, and it does not replace a clinician’s judgment.

Does the app need an account?

This page does not confirm that Letter Box AAC requires an account. If you can use a version without signing in, you do not need to create one. If a version offers an account, the privacy policy and the deletion page explain what that means and how to ask for the account or its data to be removed.

The app is not behaving as expected. What should I try?

Install the latest version from the store you used, then restart the device. If it is still stuck, write to support with the device and the version. Keep a backup way to communicate while you wait.

How do I ask for my data to be deleted?

Use the account and data deletion page. It covers information stored only on a device, and requests that go to the publisher.

Is this website the app?

No. These pages describe Letter Box AAC and hold the policies the stores ask for. Communication happens in the app.
