Privacy policy

Last updated 8 November 2026.

This policy covers the Letter Box AAC app and this website. It names a practice when that practice is part of the app. It does not name an analytics product, and it does not claim a certification or a security audit.

Who is responsible

A company legal name is not published on this site. Gage Kehoe is the developer named for Letter Box AAC.

Questions and deletion requests use the contact on the support page.

This website

These pages are static files. They do not ask you to sign in. They do not set tracking cookies. They do not load third-party analytics, advertising, or fonts. The host that serves the files may keep ordinary connection logs, such as IP address, date, and browser type, to operate and protect the site. Those logs belong to the host. They are not collected by a script on these pages.

Information the app may handle

The exact set depends on the version you install. Letter Box AAC may handle:

  • Communication content you enter, so you can use the app to communicate.
  • Settings you choose in the app.
  • Account details, such as a name or email address, if that version lets you create an account.
  • A purchase record held by Apple or Google if you download or buy through a store. This website does not receive your full payment card number.
  • Technical details a device or store can provide, such as app version, language, device type, and crash information, when that sharing is part of the version you use.

If a category does not apply to your version, the app has no need for it. This page will be updated when the list is confirmed against a shipping version. Content you enter may remain on your device. If a version also stores information on a server, for example to keep an account, that fact will be added here. This policy does not claim that information is encrypted, stored in a particular country, or covered by a named certification.

Analytics and other services

This website does not use analytics scripts. This policy does not name an analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting service inside the app, because none has been confirmed for this page. If a later version sends information to a service like that, the policy will name the service and what it receives. Until that update, ask support before treating a third-party analytics tool as part of Letter Box AAC.

Accounts

Some versions may offer an account. Some may work entirely on the device. This policy covers both. If you create an account, the account details and any communication content stored with it are information the publisher may hold. You can ask for that account and its data to be deleted from the deletion page. This website is not a login.

Children

Letter Box AAC may be used by children. A parent or guardian should decide what is entered about a child and should read this policy first. This page does not describe a verified age check, and it does not claim a children’s-privacy certification. To ask for deletion of a child’s information, use the deletion page and state that the request is for a child. Include a way to confirm you are the parent or guardian. Send only what the publisher needs to find the information.

Sharing and how long information is kept

Information is not sold. Apple or Google may process it under that store’s own policy when you download, purchase, or restore the app. If a host or other processor is used for accounts or for support email, it will be named here when it is known. A support message includes whatever you choose to write.

Information that stays on your device remains until you delete it or delete the app. Information the publisher holds, including an account and support correspondence, is kept while it is needed to provide the app, answer the request, and meet a legal duty. A retention period for named server storage will be added here if that storage is used.

Deletion

You can ask for your account and the data held with it to be deleted. The steps, what is deleted, and what may be kept are on the account and data deletion page.

Changes and contact

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. The page at this address is the current version.

A support email is not listed on this page yet. Use the developer contact shown on the Apple App Store or Google Play listing for Letter Box AAC.
