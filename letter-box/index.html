Letter Box AAC

A communication app for the person who needs another way to be heard, and for the families and clinicians beside them.

What it is

Letter Box AAC is an augmentative and alternative communication app. AAC is how people communicate when speech is unavailable, hard to understand, or not enough on its own. Letter Box takes its name from letters, a direct way to compose a message.

Who it is for

It is for the person communicating. It is also for family members, educators, and clinicians who set it up and stay with them while they use it. Letter Box AAC is a communication tool. Clinical decisions stay with the person and their clinicians.

How to get it

Letter Box AAC is being prepared for the Apple App Store and Google Play. This page is the marketing address for both listings. Listing links are not published here yet.
