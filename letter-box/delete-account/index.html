Delete an account or your data

This is the public page for Letter Box AAC account and data deletion. This website has no login. You do not need an account here to make a request.

Delete data stored only on your device

If you never created an account, what you entered can stay on that device until you remove it there.

  1. Open the device settings that list installed apps.
  2. Find Letter Box AAC.
  3. Clear the app’s storage, or delete the app.

That removes the copy on that device. It does not reach any other device you used, and it does not close an account stored with the publisher.

Ask the publisher to delete an account or stored data

Use this path if you created an account, or if you believe a copy of your data is stored off the device.

A support email is not listed on this page yet. Use the developer contact shown on the Apple App Store or Google Play listing for Letter Box AAC.

Say that you are requesting deletion, and include:

  • The app name: Letter Box AAC.
  • The email address or name on the account, if you have one.
  • Whether you installed it from Apple, Google, or both.
  • What you want removed: the account, communication content held by the publisher, or both.
  • If the request is for a child, say so, and include a way to confirm you are the parent or guardian.

Do not include a password. Include only as much of a private conversation as the publisher needs to find the right account.

What is deleted, and what can be kept

When the publisher holds an account or a copy of your data, a completed request means:

  • The account is closed, if one exists.
  • Communication content stored with that account is deleted.
  • Contact details used for the account are deleted, except a minimal record that the request was completed, or a copy a law requires the publisher to keep.

Information stays on your device until you delete the app or its storage. Apple or Google may keep their own records, such as a purchase receipt, under their policies. This request does not erase those store records.

How long it takes

The publisher completes deletion requests within 30 days, unless a law requires the information to be kept longer. You should receive a confirmation when the deletion is finished. If you do not, write again and mention the earlier request.
